Delete Your Word Board Account

Word Board players can request deletion of their Word Board account and associated personal data. You do not need to have the game installed to make a request.

Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to eagulprostudios@gmail.com, preferably from the email address linked to your Word Board account.
  2. Use the subject Word Board Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your Word Board username, if you have one. Do not send passwords, Google sign-in codes, or other sensitive credentials.
  4. Eagulpro Studios may ask you to verify account ownership before deleting the account and associated data. We will reply with next steps and confirmation when the request has been completed.
Email an account deletion request

If the button does not open your email application, email eagulprostudios@gmail.com directly.

What will be deleted?

After account ownership is verified, deletion covers the Word Board account and personal data associated with it that Eagulpro Studios controls, including, where applicable:

  • Firebase Authentication account record associated with Word Board;
  • Word Board username and linked player profile information;
  • Cloud-saved game progress, preferences, and player statistics associated with that account.

Deleting a Word Board account does not delete your Google Account. Data stored only on your device may need to be removed by clearing Word Board's app data or uninstalling the app. Google Play purchase records may remain with Google under its own policies.

Data retention

We may retain limited information only where necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, or resolving a deletion request. Any such retention will be limited to the necessary purpose and applicable requirements. We will explain any retained data and relevant retention period when responding to your request.

Delete your account inside the app

An in-app deletion option is planned for Settings → Account → Delete Account. Until that feature is available, use the email request method above. You can request deletion through this webpage even if you no longer have access to the app.

Important: This page provides a deletion-request channel. Requests are handled by Eagulpro Studios, not automatically by GitHub Pages.
