Word Board Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Eagulpro Studios ("we", "us", "our") handles information in Word Board (the "App"), a word strategy game. It applies to Word Board on Android.

1. Playing without an account

You can play Word Board as a guest without signing in. Guest gameplay, saved games, settings, and statistics may be stored locally on your device. Removing the App or clearing its data may remove locally stored progress.

2. Google Sign-In and cloud saves

If you choose to sign in with Google, the App may access your Google account identifier, display name, email address, and profile picture to authenticate you and display your player profile. We use Firebase Authentication to manage sign-in. If you enable cloud saving, Word Board may store game progress, player preferences, and statistics linked to your account in Google Cloud Firestore. Google sign-in and cloud saving are optional; guest gameplay does not require them.

3. Purchases

Word Board may offer an optional one-time Premium upgrade through Google Play Billing. Google processes payments. We do not receive your full payment-card details. We may process purchase identifiers, purchase status, and entitlement information to activate or restore Premium access and prevent fraud.

4. Technical data and service providers

Google Play services and Firebase services used by the App may process technical information necessary to operate their services, such as device or app identifiers, authentication tokens, network information, and service diagnostics, subject to their applicable terms and policies. We use this information to provide sign-in, cloud saves, purchase-related functionality, security, and reliability, as applicable to the installed version of the App.

5. Advertising and player communication

The initial version of Word Board does not display third-party advertising. If advertising is introduced in a future version, this policy and applicable app-store disclosures will be updated before that release. The initial version does not provide player-to-player messaging. If communication features are added later, we will update this policy to explain any additional information handling.

6. How information is used and shared

We use information to operate the App, authenticate optional accounts, save and restore progress, manage Premium entitlements, provide support, and protect the service. Information may be processed by service providers such as Google/Firebase and Google Play to deliver these functions. We do not sell players' personal information.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data generally remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Account-linked cloud data may remain until you request deletion or it is otherwise removed under our retention practices. To request deletion of your Word Board account and associated cloud-saved data, email eagulprostudio@gmail.com with the subject “Word Board Account Deletion.” We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing a request. We will explain any information we must retain for security, legal, or transaction-record obligations. An in-app deletion option is planned and will be described here once available.

8. Children's privacy

Word Board is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the concern and take appropriate action.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Your choices

You can play as a guest, choose whether to use Google Sign-In and cloud saves, manage local app data through your device settings, and contact us about access or deletion of account-linked information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Word Board's features or information practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
